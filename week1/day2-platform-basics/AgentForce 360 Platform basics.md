# Salesforce Platform Overview

## 1. Beyond CRM: The Platform Concept

### Standard Functionality
Salesforce provides ready-to-use tools for key business operations:

- **Sales:** Leads and Opportunities
- **Customer Service:** Cases
- **Marketing:** Marketing Cloud
- **Automation:** Flow Builder

### The "Model House" Analogy
Salesforce standard functionality is like a **model home**—you can start using it immediately.

However, the Salesforce **Platform** gives you the flexibility to customize it according to your business needs, such as:

- Painting the walls
- Adding new rooms
- Rearranging the layout

This means Salesforce is more than just a CRM—it’s a customizable business platform.

## 2. Key Standard Features

### Einstein
Salesforce’s AI-powered assistant that enhances productivity through:

- Predictive AI
- Generative AI
- Smart recommendations

### Data 360
Combines data from multiple systems to create a **single unified view of the customer**.

### Flow Builder
A no-code automation tool used to automate repetitive business workflows.

### Slack & Communities
Collaboration tools that support:

- Internal team communication
- External collaboration
- Customer/community engagement

## 3. Core Roles in Salesforce

### The User (Example: Michelle)
A person who uses Salesforce applications as part of their daily work.

**Example:**  
A real estate broker managing client relationships, opportunities, and listings.

### The Administrator (Example: D’Angelo)
A person responsible for configuring and managing the Salesforce platform.

Common responsibilities include:

- Managing users
- Creating custom functionality
- Configuring automation
- Maintaining system settings

Administrators can do most of this **without writing code**.

---

## 4. Essential Terminology (Database Basics)

Salesforce organizes data similarly to a spreadsheet or database.

| Salesforce Term | Spreadsheet Equivalent | Description |
|----------------|------------------------|-------------|
| **Org (Organization)** | Entire workbook | A private Salesforce environment for your company |
| **App** | Workspace | A collection of related objects and tools supporting a business process |
| **Object** | Table | Stores a category of information (e.g., Contacts, Properties) |
| **Field** | Column | Defines specific data attributes (e.g., Price, Address) |
| **Record** | Row | An individual data entry (e.g., a specific property listing) |
