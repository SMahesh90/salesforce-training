# Day 2: Reports and Dashboards

## 1. What is a Report?
A report is a list of facts and numbers. It shows a lot of detail about what happened in the past. It is usually written in tables or long lists. People use reports when they need to see every small detail.

## 2. What is a Dashboard?
A dashboard is a screen that uses pictures like charts and graphs. It shows the most important information in a way that is easy to see very fast. It helps people see how things are going right now.

## 3. Difference Between Reports and Dashboards

| Feature | Report | Dashboard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Look** | Lists and tables. | Charts and graphs. |
| **Detail** | Very high (lots of data). | Low (only the big points). |
| **Time** | Shows what happened before. | Shows what is happening now. |
| **Speed** | Takes time to read. | Very fast to understand. |

## 4. Principal Dashboard Design (How to make a good one)
1. **Know the User:** Make the dashboard for the person who will use it.
2. **Most Important First:** Put the big numbers at the top where people look first.
3. **Keep it Simple:** Do not put too much stuff on one screen. Keep it clean.
4. **Use Right Charts:** Use easy charts, like bar charts or line charts.
5. **Use Filters:** Let the user change the dates or categories easily.

## 5. Five Important College Reports
1. **Enrollment Report:** A list of how many new students joined.
2. **Grade Report:** A look at the grades students are getting in their classes.
3. **Attendance Report:** A list showing which students are coming to class.
4. **Fees Report:** A report showing which students have paid their school fees and who still pending.
5. **Graduation Report:** A list of how many students finished their degree on time.

## 6. Reflection: How does data help management make decisions?
Data helps managers stop guessing. Instead of "thinking" something is true, they can "see" the truth. 

For example, data shows them:
*   Where they are wasting money.
*   If they need to hire more people.
*   Which students need extra help.
*   What might happen in the future based on the past.
